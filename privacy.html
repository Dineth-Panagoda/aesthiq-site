Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Aesthiq app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Dineth Panagoda (collectively, the "Application"). Dineth Panagoda is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • The screens of the Application you view, the features you use, and the time, date, and duration of your activity
  • Your mobile device type and the operating system you use
  • A randomly generated, anonymous user identifier used to associate your activity, subscription status, and edits with your installation of the Application. This identifier is not linked to your name or email address.
  • Your subscription and purchase status
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • Photographs that you choose to upload, and any style reference images you select, in order to generate edited images

The Application uses anonymous authentication. It does not require you to create an account or to provide your name or email address in order to use its core features.


Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery. The Application uses first-party analytics tied to the anonymous user identifier described above; it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not use the device advertising identifier (IDFA). Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.


Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the Service Provider. To exercise these rights, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, contact the Service Provider at dinethchandimal@gmail.com.


Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. The Service Provider does not sell your personal information. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact the Service Provider at dinethchandimal@gmail.com.


Artificial Intelligence

The Application uses artificial intelligence (AI) to create edited versions of your photographs. When you request an edit, the photograph you provide — together with any style reference image and processing instructions — is transmitted to Google's Gemini API, which generates the edited image and returns it to the Application. This processing is necessary to provide the core functionality of the Application.

Your photographs are sent to Google solely to perform the requested edit. The Service Provider does not retain your original photographs on its servers beyond what is necessary to process your request, and does not use your photographs to train its own models. Google's handling of images and data sent to the Gemini API is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy, linked in the Third Party Access section below. Edited images are stored locally on your device so that you can access your recent edits, and remain under your control; deleting the Application removes them.


The Service Provider may use the information you provide to send important information and required notices. The Application can also send push notifications, including, where permitted by law, occasional notifications about new features or content. You can turn push notifications off at any time within the Application's settings or in your device settings.


For a better experience while using the Application, the Service Provider may process certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to an anonymous user identifier and the photographs you choose to upload. The information the Service Provider processes will be retained and used as described in this privacy policy.

To provide and improve the Application, the Service Provider shares certain information with the third-party service providers listed below.
