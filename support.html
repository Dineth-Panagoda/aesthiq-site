Subscriptions and billing

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us, so cancelling happens in your device settings:

  1. Open the Settings app
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select Aesthiq, then Cancel Subscription

Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date, or that period will still be charged. After cancelling you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

How do I cancel during the free trial?

The same steps apply. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged. If you cancel during the trial you keep access until the trial period finishes.

I already paid but the app says I'm not subscribed

Open the paywall in the app and tap Restore Purchases. This re-checks your Apple ID for an active subscription. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you purchased with. If it still doesn't restore, email us and include the Apple ID email used for the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

All purchases go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple rather than by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple ID that made the purchase. If Apple declines and you believe there's been a genuine error, get in touch and we'll help where we can.

Edits and credits

How does the free tier work?

New installs come with 5 starter edits. Once those are used, you get one free edit per day, which resets at midnight UTC. Subscribing removes the daily limit.

Why does my saved photo have a watermark?

Edits saved on the free tier carry a small Aesthiq watermark. Subscribing removes it from all future saves. It isn't applied retroactively, so re-run an edit after subscribing to get a clean version.

My edit failed or timed out

Image generation runs on external AI infrastructure that occasionally gets busy. If an edit fails:

  • Wait a moment and try again — most failures are temporary load, not a problem with your photo
  • Check your internet connection; edits need a stable connection for as long as the generation runs
  • If a free edit or credit was consumed by a failed generation, it's refunded automatically. If your balance still looks wrong, email us

The result doesn't look how I expected

Results vary with lighting, resolution, and how much of the frame the subject fills. Photos that are well lit and reasonably sharp give the most consistent results. Try a different style variation, or adjust the style strength on the result screen.

Your photos and data

Are my photos stored on your servers?

No. To create an edit, your photo is sent to Google's Gemini API, which generates the edited image and returns it. We don't retain your original photographs on our servers beyond what's needed to process that request, and we don't use your photos to train models. Your edits are stored locally on your device, and deleting the app removes them.

Do I need an account?

No. Aesthiq uses anonymous authentication — there's no sign-up, and we never ask for your name or email address to use the app.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device and stops any further collection. To request deletion of data already held on our side, email dinethchandimal@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Device requirements

Aesthiq requires iPhone running iOS 18.0 or later. It isn't currently available for iPad or Mac.
