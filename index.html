What it does

Built for people whose feed has a look.

Aesthiq is made for content creators and photographers who want every post to belong to the same visual world — not a folder of one-off filters.

Find your aesthetic

Browse a curated catalog of looks — Y2K digicam flash, disposable camera, warm studio portrait, and more. Each one is a full grade built to hold up across a whole set of photos, not a single-tap filter that falls apart on the next shot. New looks land every week.

Match any aesthetic

Got a mood no preset covers? Drop in a reference photo and Aesthiq reads its colour, contrast and light, then applies that same treatment to your own shot. Any image you've saved becomes a look you can reuse.

Keep your feed consistent

Every edit is saved to your library with a before-and-after view, so you can see the through-line across a set at a glance and reapply the same look to the next photo in seconds.

Tune every detail yourself

The AI grade is a starting point, not a verdict. Dial the style amount up or down, then reach for a full set of manual sliders — exposure, contrast, temperature, grain and more — until it's exactly yours.

Stack frames & film effects

Finish with instant-camera frames, date stamps, film grain and light leaks. Layer them over any grade to land the exact era you're going for.

How it works

Three steps. That's the whole app.

STEP 01

Choose your photo

Select any photo from your camera roll.

STEP 02

Pick a look

Dozens of curated looks — from Y2K digicam flash to studio portraits.

STEP 03

Get the look

Aesthiq regrades your photo like a colorist would. Fine-tune with sliders and overlays.

✦ Under the hood, Aesthiq runs on Google's Gemini models for image understanding and grading, with on-device rendering for the manual adjustments so edits stay instant.
